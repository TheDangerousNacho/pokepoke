# Real screenshot samples

Drop real Pokémon GO screenshots here and they will be used to tune OCR.

Gitignored — nothing in here is committed, so screenshots stay on your machine.

Useful cases, roughly in order:
1. A Pokémon with its **default name** (CP reads reliably?)
2. A **renamed** Pokémon (does the CP+HP shortlist find it?)
3. One with a **light background** behind the CP, and one with a dark/saturated one
4. Anything the scanner got wrong — those are the most useful of all
